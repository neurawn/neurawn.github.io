## Deriving the RLHF Objective

**Policy** $$\pi_\theta(y \mid x)$$: probability that the model produces $$y$$ given $$x$$.

**Reward:** $$r(x, y) \;\Rightarrow\;$$ avg score:

$$
\sum_{y} \pi_\theta(y \mid x)\, r(x, y)
$$

which is also

$$
= \mathbb{E}_{y \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot \mid x)}\big[ r(x, y) \big]
$$

### General Structure

$$
\text{Objective} = \text{avg predicted reward} - \beta \times \text{cost to change behavior}
$$

### Changing Behavior

$$
\frac{\pi_\theta(y \mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y \mid x)}
\quad
\begin{array}{l}
\leftarrow \text{our behavior} \\
\leftarrow \text{ref behavior}
\end{array}
$$

This tells us how much more or less likely it is that the new model makes the new behavior happen.

- Take a log and average over all answers generated by the new model:

$$
\sum_{y} \pi_\theta(y \mid x) \log \frac{\pi_\theta(y \mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y \mid x)}
$$

which is also the KL divergence

$$
= D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big( \pi_\theta \,\|\, \pi_{\text{ref}} \big)
$$
